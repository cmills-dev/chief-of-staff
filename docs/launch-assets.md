## On-screen story

1. Chief of Staff. A night shift for everything you run.
2. Your days run on a lot of moving parts. And some of them slip.
3. At 02:30, Chief of Staff starts its shift.
4. First, a read of the whole day.
5. Then, what needs you rises to the top.
6. Anything safe to move forward gets done, with an undo.
7. Finished threads get closed, on proof.
8. At 07:00, one note is waiting.
9. And every Sunday, the bigger picture. What to double down on. What to drop.
10. Go to sleep. Wake up sorted.

The film uses illustrative interface compositions and simplified examples. It is not a live screen recording. The README describes the boundaries of the installed system, including actions for which undo is unavailable.
